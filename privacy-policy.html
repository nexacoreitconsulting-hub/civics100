Privacy Policy

Civics 100 (Android app) · Developer: Nexacore · Effective date: October 9, 2026

In short: Civics 100 does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app works offline, and your study progress and practice scores stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app useful, the following is saved only on your device:

  • Which questions you have marked as “mastered”
  • Your practice interview scores and the date and time of each attempt

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can clear your score history at any time with the “Clear history” button in the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s Settings or uninstalling the app.

3. Device backups

If you have turned on Android’s backup feature, Android may include the app’s on-device data (your progress and scores) in your personal Google account backup. This is controlled by your device settings and by Google, not by us, and we have no access to those backups.

4. Internet access, ads, and third parties

The app does not require an internet connection, shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases, and does not send data to us or to any third party. If the app ever displays a link to an outside website, that site is governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is designed for adults preparing for the U.S. naturalization civics test and is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Not a government app

Civics 100 is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or any government agency. Question content is based on publicly available USCIS civics study materials.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Changes that affect how data is handled will also be reflected in the app’s Google Play listing.
